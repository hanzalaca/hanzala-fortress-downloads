  • إذا سمحت بالتنبيهات، يرسل التطبيق إلى خادمنا رمز تنبيهات خاصاً بجهازك (من Apple على الآيفون، ومن خدمة Expo على أندرويد)، مع اختياراتك: اللغة والمدن والمواضيع وإعدادات التنبيهات وفرق التوقيت، والأخبار التي تتابعها. نستخدمها فقط لإرسال التنبيهات التي اخترتها.

  • على الآيفون يرسل خادمنا التنبيهات مباشرة عبر خدمة تنبيهات Apple. على أندرويد تمر عبر خدمة Expo ثم Google.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

This policy covers the HNIS app on iPhone and Android.

No accounts, no tracking

  • There are no accounts and no logins.
  • There are no analytics, tracking tools or ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your choices (language, cities and topics) are stored on your device.

Alerts

  • If you allow notifications, the app sends our server a push token for your device (from Apple on iPhone, from the Expo service on Android) together with your choices: language, cities, topics, alert settings, time-zone offset, and the stories you follow. We use them only to send the alerts you chose.
  • The token does not contain your name, number or email. If you turn notifications off or delete the app, the token stops working.
  • On iPhone, our server sends alerts directly through Apple's push notification service. On Android, they pass through the Expo service and then Google.

What reaches our server

  • When you open the news, the app requests it from our server. Our hosting provider may keep temporary technical logs such as IP addresses to protect the service.
  • We do not sell or share any data.
