# Privacy Policy — HANZALA FORTRESS

Effective date: 2026-10-24

This policy covers the HANZALA FORTRESS app on iPhone, Android and Mac.

## We collect no data

- The app collects no data about you and sends nothing anywhere.
- There are no accounts and no logins.
- There are no analytics, tracking tools or ads.

## Your files stay on your device

- Every photo and file you add is encrypted on your own device.
- Files never leave the device unless you export them yourself, by sharing a file or saving a copy of the vault.
- Your password and recovery key never reach us. We cannot open your vault or recover your password.
- The camera is used only to take photos that go straight into the encrypted vault. Face ID or fingerprint unlock is optional and works on the device only.

## Purchases

- The iPhone subscription is handled entirely by Apple.
- The developer receives no personal or payment information about you. What you share with Apple is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
