سياسة الخصوصية

Support

HANZALA FORTRESS is an encrypted on-device vault for your photos and files. No account, no login.

Forgot your password

On the unlock screen tap “Use your recovery key” and enter the key shown when you created the vault. Only you have the password and the key; we cannot recover them.

Subscription

HANZALA FORTRESS Pro is a yearly subscription through Apple, with a 3-day free trial. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If it ends, all your files stay available to you.
